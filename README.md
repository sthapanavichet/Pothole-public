## Project Description

Road damage is a daily safety and cost issue for drivers, cyclists, delivery workers, and municipalities. People often report potholes through disconnected channels, and city teams may not receive enough detail to prioritize repairs quickly. This creates delays, repeat complaints, and frustration for residents who feel their reports disappear without updates.

This project provides a simple platform that helps people report potholes with clear location details, photos, and severity notes. Instead of relying on unstructured messages, constant data is collected and the system organizes submissions so municipal teams or road maintenance partners can review high-impact issues first.

At a high level, the platform acts as a bridge between identification and the maintenance teams. Teams can manage incoming requests in one place. The goal is to improve visibility, reduce response time, and make road repair planning more transparent (and faster) for everyone involved.
